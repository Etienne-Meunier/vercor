# VerCOR Module Dependency Order

The numbered entries below are topological import layers for the current source
tree. A module may import modules in its own layer only where an explicit
strongly connected pair is documented; otherwise production imports point to an
earlier layer. Imports guarded only by `TYPE_CHECKING` are annotation edges and
are not runtime-order constraints. Public owner facades are listed separately
from their concrete private implementations.

1. `vercor/_field_names.py`, `vercor/_interpolators/_bilinear_geometry.py`, `vercor/_logging/__init__.py`, `vercor/_logging/config.py`, `vercor/_pytree.py`, `vercor/_regridders/__init__.py`, `vercor/_run_order.py`, `vercor/_runtime/__init__.py`, `vercor/_runtime/interrupts.py`, `vercor/calendar.py`, `vercor/dtypes.py`, `vercor/exceptions.py`, `vercor/fields.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/__init__.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/__init__.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_contracts.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_imports.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_runtime_settings.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/veros_runtime_settings.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/veros_setup.py`, `vercor/setups/_lazy_imports.py`, `vercor/setups/_time_helpers.py`, and `vercor/types.py` - independent foundations: public field/type/dtype/calendar/error contracts, shared private text-safe name-sequence freezing, private tuple/PyTree/logging/runtime package bases, and optional-adapter configuration/import boundaries.
2. `vercor/_host_arrays.py`, `vercor/_interpolators/_bilinear_extrapolation.py`, `vercor/_interpolators/_bilinear_weights.py`, `vercor/_interpolators/conservative_remap_rectilinear.py`, `vercor/_logging/protocols.py`, `vercor/_runtime/component_topology.py`, `vercor/_runtime/stores.py`, `vercor/_runtime/topology_state.py`, `vercor/assets.py`, `vercor/clock.py`, `vercor/field_layout.py`, `vercor/forcing_data.py`, `vercor/forcing_index.py`, `vercor/grids.py`, `vercor/physics.py`, `vercor/recipes.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/_jax_gcm_pytree.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_forcing.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/jax_gcm_tools.py`, and `vercor/time_selection.py` - first consumers of the foundations, including the private host/PyTree/interpolation mechanics and the canonical public `Clock`, `RectilinearGrid`, and `PhysicalConstants` owners.
3. `vercor/_interpolators/bilinear_rectilinear.py`, `vercor/_logging/host.py`, `vercor/_runtime/state.py`, `vercor/_runtime/time.py`, `vercor/components/setup_validation.py`, `vercor/fluxes/utilities.py`, `vercor/fluxes/vertical_coordinates.py`, `vercor/grid_geometry.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/_field_helpers.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/assets.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_tensors.py`, and `vercor/setups/_external/veros_state.py` - private numerical/runtime implementations and public grid-geometry/flux utilities built from layers 1-2.
4. `vercor/_interpolators/__init__.py`, `vercor/_logging/callback.py`, `vercor/_regridders/base.py`, `vercor/fluxes/bulk_formula_cesm.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_fields.py`, and `vercor/setups/_external/jax_gcm_fields.py` - concrete private interpolation, logging, regridder, flux, and adapter-field implementation owners.
5. `vercor/_regridders/bilinear.py`, `vercor/_regridders/conservative.py`, `vercor/fluxes/__init__.py`, `vercor/jax_logging.py`, and `vercor/setups/_external/veros_fluxes.py` - concrete private regridders precede the later public regridding facade; `vercor.fluxes` and `vercor.jax_logging` are the public owners over their focused implementations.
6. `vercor/_runtime/progress.py`, `vercor/components/contexts.py`, `vercor/regridding.py`, and `vercor/setups/_external/_camulator_wind_filtering.py` - public setup/step contexts and `Regridder`/`VectorRegridder`/`RegridderFactory` plus `bilinear(...)`/`conservative(...)` facade, built after the concrete private regridders; private runtime progress and wind-filter mechanics remain non-public.
7. `vercor/exchanges.py`, `vercor/grid_masks.py`, and `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_wind_filter.py` - canonical public `Exchange` owner, public mask utilities, and the adapter wind-filter facade. Exchange field aliases remain owned solely by `vercor.fields`; this layer consumes them.
8. `vercor/_runtime/contracts.py` and `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_stepper.py` - private exchange-contract and CAMulator stepping implementations. Runtime consumers use `Exchange.route_id` directly as the sole route identity.
9. `vercor/_runtime/dispatch_context.py`, `vercor/_runtime/exchange_topology.py`, `vercor/_runtime/field_transfer.py`, and `vercor/state.py` - private dispatch/topology/transfer implementations plus the canonical public `RunState` and `ComponentState` owner.
10. `vercor/_runtime/exchange_dispatch.py`, `vercor/components/_protocol.py`, and `vercor/diagnostics/fields.py` - destination exchange dispatch, the dependency-light structural component protocol, and state-backed diagnostic fields. The protocol is internally owned but reports `vercor.components.contracts` as its canonical public module; one import-finalization binding resolves its `ComponentSpec` forward annotation after declarations load.
11. `vercor/output/__init__.py` - stable output contracts (`OutputProvider`, frames, specs, targets, variables, cadence, and snapshot contexts) depend only on the component protocol, not the full component declaration module. The former component/output runtime import cycle is removed.
12. `vercor/components/contracts.py`, `vercor/components/_callable_wrappers.py`, `vercor/components/_contracts.py`, `vercor/components/data.py`, `vercor/diagnostics/plotting.py`, `vercor/diagnostics/tables.py`, `vercor/output/_netcdf.py`, `vercor/output/_period.py`, and `vercor/setups/config.py` - component declarations and normalization helpers, `DataComponent`, diagnostics, shared output math/file primitives, and frozen setup-specific configuration.
13. `vercor/_runtime/validation.py`, `vercor/components/_adapter.py`, `vercor/components/_runtime_fields.py`, `vercor/components/base.py`, `vercor/diagnostics/__init__.py`, `vercor/output/_dataset.py`, and `vercor/output/_runtime.py` - private validation/binding/output implementations, the public `CallableComponent` implementation, the diagnostics facade, and final runtime-view/snapshot writing.
14. `vercor/_runtime/component_state.py`, `vercor/_runtime/driver.py`, `vercor/_runtime/state_validation.py`, `vercor/components/__init__.py`, `vercor/components/runtime_execution.py`, `vercor/output/_session.py`, the strongly connected pair `vercor/_runtime/surface_masks.py` plus `vercor/topology.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/_component_helpers.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/jcm_land.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_land.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_output.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/jax_gcm_output.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/veros_output.py`, `vercor/setups/_slab/atmosphere.py`, `vercor/setups/_slab/land.py`, `vercor/setups/_slab/ocean.py`, and `vercor/setups/_slab/seaice.py` - runtime state/driver validation, the public component facade, the sole immutable period-output plan/session, topology policy, bundled native providers, data helpers, and slab components. There is no component-output adapter or second period-file lifecycle.
15. `vercor/_runtime/coupler_state.py`, `vercor/_runtime/topology_policy.py`, `vercor/runtime/__init__.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/era5_atmosphere.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/era5_land.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/era5_ocean.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/erainterim_ocean.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_init.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_runtime.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/jax_gcm_runtime.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/veros_gcm_state.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/veros_runtime.py`, and `vercor/setups/_slab/__init__.py` - public `RuntimeOptions`/execution-extension owner after its clock, state, logging, and topology dependencies; private runtime topology/state and concrete setup runtimes.
16. `vercor/_runtime/run_context.py`, `vercor/_runtime/topology.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_gcm_state.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/jax_gcm_state.py`, and `vercor/setups/_external/veros_gcm.py` - frozen run inputs, topology orchestration, and external setup-state/factory implementations.
17. `vercor/_runtime/backends.py`, `vercor/_runtime/initialization.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator.py`, and `vercor/setups/_external/jax_gcm.py` - run-wide clock metadata, schedule-cached JIT-scanned executors, host-driver and custom chunk backend execution, one-shot component preparation and topology initialization, and lazily invoked external factories. Supplied, pre-driver, and backend-returned states, together with per-component driver results, are checked against the private prepared binding for exact component/store/route names, grid type/name/coordinates/edges/masks, array shapes and dtypes, and finite binary/fractional mask constraints.
18. `vercor/_runtime/execution.py`, `vercor/_runtime/prepared.py`, and `vercor/setups/_jcm.py` - validated workflow planning/chunk coordination, one frozen private prepared-coupling boundary, and paired JCM setup ownership. The execution coordinator selects backends only from scheduled components and retains core ownership of output and cancellation boundaries. Preparation retains private frozen component bindings without a reflective configuration snapshot or public prepared graph.
19. `vercor/_runtime/preparation.py`, `vercor/_runtime/runner.py`, and `vercor/setups/__init__.py` - runtime-state creation/validation, interrupt-scoped delegation to workflow execution, and the sole public lazy setup export table, after all eagerly imported slab/JCM factories.
20. `vercor/_runtime/facade.py` - high-level private orchestration for preparation reuse, runtime execution, explicit output-target validation, final-field output, and snapshots.
21. `vercor/coupler.py` - sole canonical public `Coupler` owner, placed after its private runtime facade. Constructor assembly owns immutable collection/declaration snapshots while public `components` retains the original author objects; those objects are treated as immutable configuration. `initial_state(*, prefill_missing=True)` and `run(state=None, *, output=None)` are the only runtime boundaries; `run_order=()` performs setup/state preparation without advancing a component.
22. `vercor/__init__.py` - final primary convenience facade exporting exactly `Clock`, `Coupler`, `Exchange`, `RectilinearGrid`, `RunState`, and `RuntimeOptions`, in that order.
23. `examples/` - runnable constructor-only setup scripts consuming the public owners and `vercor.setups`; examples retain prepared states and do not depend on removed primary modules.
24. `pyproject.toml`, `vercor/py.typed`, `.github/workflows/python-package.yml`, `tests/fixtures/public_plugin/`, and `tests/fixtures/public_plugin_0_3/` - VerCOR 0.4.0a1 packaging, PEP 561 typing, wheel/installed-sdist and Python 3.12/3.13 base/JCM/Veros CI, native 0.4 plugin lanes, historical frozen-0.3 artifact inspection, and the macOS smoke. The 0.4 plugin composes frozen configuration/factory, structural regridding, route-keyed topology, workflow/backend, period/snapshot output, and immutable state replacement. The frozen 0.3 fixture is not executed against 0.4.
25. `README.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `docs/api-architecture-review.md`, `docs/migration-0.3-to-0.4.md`, and `docs/releasing.md` - runnable public quick starts, exact eight-section architecture review and complete inventory, direct migration guidance, release history, and candidate verification procedure, built on the implemented layers above.
26. `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-13-vercor-0.4-api-design.md`, `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-13-vercor-0.4-api.md`, `tests/contracts/vercor-0.3.2-public-api.json`, `tests/test_v0_4_compatibility_baseline.py`, `tests/test_api_architecture_review.py`, `tests/test_distribution_boundaries.py`, and `tests/test_final_review_boundaries.py` - approved design/plan, immutable 0.3.2 historical baseline, executable documentation/manifests/signatures/snippets, installed artifact boundaries, and focused final-review regressions. Tasks 1-8 plus Task 10 form this alpha; Task 9 was explicitly skipped and no legacy adapter namespace is present. Final local evidence is 522 fast tests, 1109 full tests, 90.49% branch coverage, 16 distribution-boundary tests from fresh source-built artifacts, 9 JCM/Veros focused cases, and 3 output-free JVP/reverse cases.
