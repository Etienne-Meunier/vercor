# VerCOR Module Dependency Order

The numbered entries below are topological import layers for the current source
tree. A module may import modules in its own layer only where an explicit
strongly connected pair is documented; otherwise production imports point to an
earlier layer. Imports guarded only by `TYPE_CHECKING` are annotation edges and
are not runtime-order constraints. Public owner facades are listed separately
from their concrete private implementations.

1. `vercor/_field_names.py`, `vercor/_interpolators/_bilinear_geometry.py`, `vercor/_logging/__init__.py`, `vercor/_logging/config.py`, `vercor/_pytree.py`, `vercor/_regridders/__init__.py`, `vercor/_run_order.py`, `vercor/_runtime/__init__.py`, `vercor/_runtime/interrupts.py`, `vercor/calendar.py`, `vercor/dtypes.py`, `vercor/exceptions.py`, `vercor/fields.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/__init__.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/__init__.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_contracts.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_imports.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_runtime_settings.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/veros_runtime_settings.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/veros_setup.py`, `vercor/setups/_lazy_imports.py`, `vercor/setups/_time_helpers.py`, and `vercor/types.py` - independent foundations: public field/type/dtype/calendar/error contracts, private tuple/PyTree/logging/runtime package bases, and optional-adapter configuration/import boundaries.
2. `vercor/_host_arrays.py`, `vercor/_interpolators/_bilinear_extrapolation.py`, `vercor/_interpolators/_bilinear_weights.py`, `vercor/_interpolators/conservative_remap_rectilinear.py`, `vercor/_logging/protocols.py`, `vercor/_runtime/component_topology.py`, `vercor/_runtime/stores.py`, `vercor/_runtime/topology_state.py`, `vercor/assets.py`, `vercor/clock.py`, `vercor/field_layout.py`, `vercor/forcing_data.py`, `vercor/forcing_index.py`, `vercor/grids.py`, `vercor/physics.py`, `vercor/recipes.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/_jax_gcm_pytree.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_forcing.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/jax_gcm_tools.py`, and `vercor/time_selection.py` - first consumers of the foundations, including the private host/PyTree/interpolation mechanics and the canonical public `Clock`, `RectilinearGrid`, and `PhysicalConstants` owners.
3. `vercor/_interpolators/bilinear_rectilinear.py`, `vercor/_logging/host.py`, `vercor/_runtime/state.py`, `vercor/_runtime/time.py`, `vercor/components/setup_validation.py`, `vercor/fluxes/utilities.py`, `vercor/fluxes/vertical_coordinates.py`, `vercor/grid_geometry.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/_field_helpers.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/assets.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_tensors.py`, and `vercor/setups/_external/veros_state.py` - private numerical/runtime implementations and public grid-geometry/flux utilities built from layers 1-2.
4. `vercor/_interpolators/__init__.py`, `vercor/_logging/callback.py`, `vercor/_regridders/base.py`, `vercor/fluxes/bulk_formula_cesm.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_fields.py`, and `vercor/setups/_external/jax_gcm_fields.py` - concrete private interpolation, logging, regridder, flux, and adapter-field implementation owners.
5. `vercor/_regridders/bilinear.py`, `vercor/_regridders/conservative.py`, `vercor/fluxes/__init__.py`, `vercor/jax_logging.py`, and `vercor/setups/_external/veros_fluxes.py` - concrete private regridders precede the later public regridding facade; `vercor.fluxes` and `vercor.jax_logging` are the public owners over their focused implementations.
6. `vercor/_runtime/progress.py`, `vercor/components/contexts.py`, `vercor/regridding.py`, and `vercor/setups/_external/_camulator_wind_filtering.py` - public setup/step contexts and `Regridder`/`VectorRegridder`/`RegridderFactory` plus `bilinear(...)`/`conservative(...)` facade, built after the concrete private regridders; private runtime progress and wind-filter mechanics remain non-public.
7. `vercor/exchanges.py`, `vercor/grid_masks.py`, and `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_wind_filter.py` - canonical public `Exchange` owner, public mask utilities, and the adapter wind-filter facade. Exchange field aliases remain owned solely by `vercor.fields`; this layer consumes them.
8. `vercor/_runtime/contracts.py` and `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_stepper.py` - private exchange-contract and CAMulator stepping implementations. Runtime consumers use `Exchange.route_id` directly as the sole route identity.
9. `vercor/_runtime/dispatch_context.py`, `vercor/_runtime/exchange_topology.py`, `vercor/_runtime/field_transfer.py`, and `vercor/state.py` - private dispatch/topology/transfer implementations plus the canonical public `RunState` and `ComponentState` owner.
10. `vercor/_runtime/exchange_dispatch.py`, the strongly connected pair `vercor/components/contracts.py` plus `vercor/output/__init__.py`, and `vercor/diagnostics/fields.py` - destination exchange dispatch, the current component/output declaration cycle, and state-backed diagnostic fields. `ComponentSpec.output` and `SnapshotContext.component` are the only documented component/output cycle; Task 7 owns separating the declarations from runtime output contexts.
11. `vercor/components/_callable_wrappers.py`, `vercor/components/_contracts.py`, `vercor/components/data.py`, `vercor/diagnostics/plotting.py`, `vercor/diagnostics/tables.py`, `vercor/output/_netcdf.py`, `vercor/output/_period.py`, and `vercor/setups/config.py` - component normalization helpers and `DataComponent`, diagnostics, current output primitives, and frozen setup-specific configuration.
12. `vercor/_runtime/validation.py`, `vercor/components/_adapter.py`, `vercor/components/_runtime_fields.py`, `vercor/components/base.py`, `vercor/diagnostics/__init__.py`, `vercor/output/_dataset.py`, `vercor/output/_period_files.py`, and `vercor/output/_runtime.py` - private validation/binding/output implementations, the public `CallableComponent` implementation, and the public diagnostics facade. Final output currently uses component-registered snapshot-writer declarations and direct runtime-view NetCDF output; no Task 7 provider/target API is claimed.
13. `vercor/_runtime/state_validation.py`, `vercor/components/__init__.py`, `vercor/components/runtime_execution.py`, `vercor/output/_component_adapter.py`, and `vercor/output/_session.py` - public component-author facade over the private binding, plus runtime execution and current component-aware output-session implementations.
14. `vercor/_runtime/component_state.py`, `vercor/_runtime/driver.py`, the strongly connected pair `vercor/_runtime/surface_masks.py` plus `vercor/topology.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/_component_helpers.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/jcm_land.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_land.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_output.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/jax_gcm_output.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/veros_output.py`, `vercor/setups/_slab/atmosphere.py`, `vercor/setups/_slab/land.py`, `vercor/setups/_slab/ocean.py`, and `vercor/setups/_slab/seaice.py` - runtime state/driver ownership, the current topology-policy cycle, output adapters, data helpers, and slab component implementations.
15. `vercor/_runtime/coupler_state.py`, `vercor/_runtime/topology_policy.py`, `vercor/runtime/__init__.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/era5_atmosphere.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/era5_land.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/era5_ocean.py`, `vercor/setups/_data/erainterim_ocean.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_init.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_runtime.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/jax_gcm_runtime.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/veros_gcm_state.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/veros_runtime.py`, and `vercor/setups/_slab/__init__.py` - public `RuntimeOptions`/execution-extension owner after its clock, state, logging, and topology dependencies; private runtime topology/state and concrete setup runtimes.
16. `vercor/_runtime/run_context.py`, `vercor/_runtime/topology.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator_gcm_state.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/jax_gcm_state.py`, and `vercor/setups/_external/veros_gcm.py` - frozen run inputs, topology orchestration, and external setup-state/factory implementations.
17. `vercor/_runtime/backends.py`, `vercor/_runtime/initialization.py`, `vercor/setups/_external/camulator.py`, and `vercor/setups/_external/jax_gcm.py` - host/scanned/custom backend execution, one-shot component preparation and topology initialization, and lazily invoked external factories. Supplied, pre-driver, and backend-returned states are checked against the private prepared binding for exact component/store/route names, grid type/name/coordinates/edges/masks, array shapes and dtypes, and finite binary/fractional mask constraints.
18. `vercor/_runtime/prepared.py`, `vercor/_runtime/runner.py`, and `vercor/setups/_jcm.py` - one frozen private prepared-coupling boundary, run-mode selection, and paired JCM setup ownership. Preparation retains private frozen component bindings without a reflective configuration snapshot or public prepared graph.
19. `vercor/_runtime/preparation.py` and `vercor/setups/__init__.py` - runtime-state creation/validation and the sole public lazy setup export table, after all eagerly imported slab/JCM factories.
20. `vercor/_runtime/facade.py` - high-level private orchestration for preparation reuse, runtime execution, and transitional output delegation.
21. `vercor/coupler.py` - sole canonical public `Coupler` owner, placed after its private runtime facade. Constructor assembly owns immutable collection/declaration snapshots while public `components` retains the original author objects; those objects are treated as immutable configuration. `initial_state(*, prefill_missing=True)`, `run()`, and `write_outputs()` remain the current transitional boundaries, and `run_order=()` performs setup/state preparation without advancing a component.
22. `vercor/__init__.py` - final primary convenience facade exporting exactly `Clock`, `Coupler`, `Exchange`, `RectilinearGrid`, `RunState`, and `RuntimeOptions`, in that order.
23. `examples/` - runnable constructor-only setup scripts consuming the public owners and `vercor.setups`; examples retain prepared states and do not depend on removed primary modules.
24. `pyproject.toml`, `vercor/py.typed`, `.github/workflows/python-package.yml`, `tests/fixtures/public_plugin/`, and `tests/fixtures/public_plugin_3_0/` - transitional packaging, typing, CI, current constructor-only plugin, and frozen 3.0 compatibility evidence.
25. `README.md` and `docs/api-architecture-review.md` - current public-only quick starts plus the historical VerCOR 3.1.1 review retained as Task 10 rewrite input, built on the implemented layers above.
26. `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-13-vercor-4-api-design.md`, `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-13-vercor-4-api.md`, `tests/contracts/vercor-3.1.1-public-api.json`, `tests/test_v4_compatibility_baseline.py`, and `tests/test_distribution_boundaries.py` - approved future design/plan, immutable 3.1.1 baseline, and artifact-boundary evidence. Task 5 implements stable route IDs, scalar/vector regridder capabilities, route-keyed build-only topology, and strict opaque state alignment. Task 6 workflows, Task 7 unified output providers/targets, Task 9 `vercor.compat.v3`, and the Task 10 version/release rewrite remain future work.
